# Q3 Interiors ad rules

These apply to every ad the studio builds. The brand values are in `ads/kit/ad.css`; the reasons are in the brand section of `CLAUDE.md`.

1. **Black and white.** White or black backgrounds, ink `#171717` text, muted `#555555` for small print. No other colours, gradients or effects. Real project photos keep their own colours.
2. **The wordmark is artwork.** Use `kit:wordmark-black.svg` on white and `kit:wordmark-white.svg` on black (or the compact Q, `kit:q-black.svg` / `kit:q-white.svg`). Never redraw, recolour, stretch, crop, outline or add effects. Keep clear space around it of at least 12px at 160px wide (scale up with size).
3. **Words.** Sentence case. Irish English. The business is "Q3 Interiors" in text; "Q THREE" exists only in the artwork. Use the site's vocabulary: enquiry, site visit, estimate (a price range), quote (an agreed scope). The main action is "Tell us about your project".
4. **Only what is true and approved.** No prices, dates, reviews, coverage areas, credentials, awards or guarantees unless Q3 has confirmed them and they are on the site. When in doubt, leave it out and ask Chris.
5. **Photos.** Only approved Q3 project photos, handed in by Chris. Until there are some, ads are type-only.
6. **Every ad is built in two sizes:** feed 4:5 (1440 x 1800) and Stories/Reels 9:16 (1440 x 2560). Square (1440 x 1440) only when asked. Text, logo and key content stay inside `.safe`: on 9:16 Instagram covers the top 14% and the bottom 35%.
7. **Self-contained.** One HTML file per size. Fonts, logo and images are inlined when rendered; a published file that refers to anything outside itself is refused. No scripts, video, audio or SVG animation in any ad.
8. **Short copy.** Headline up to 40 characters, button label up to 30 (use one of Instagram's buttons, usually "Learn more" or "Contact us"). Caption: the first 125 characters carry it; up to 2,200 allowed.
9. **Animation is subtle and optional.** An animated ad declares `duration_ms` in `ad.json` (1 to 15 seconds, a multiple of 100 ms) and moves with CSS animations only, in a loop of that length (`--loop` on `.ad`). The message is complete and readable on the last frame, and either on the first frame too or, for a build-in (rule 10), within the first 3 seconds, after which it holds unchanged for at least half the loop; nothing essential appears only mid-loop. The last frame is the poster and must match the still version of the same ad. Move the rules, a reveal, a small shift; never flash, blink, spin or move the words fast. Everything stays inside `.safe` at every frame (the render checks each one). A still ad has no `duration_ms` and nothing moves.
10. **Bold black** (`templates/bold-black`, Chris's art direction of 2026-11-26). A black canvas with generous left-aligned margins; at the top a small letter-spaced "Q THREE INTERIORS" line (Roboto 500, 64px, tracked 0.1em) over a thin white rule; one very large, tight headline in Roboto 700 (160px on the feed, 90% of that on 9:16) set in two to four chosen lines with no single-word last line unless every line is one word; a short supporting line in Roboto 400 white (64px), also broken by hand where it helps; one full-width white rounded button at the bottom with a black label and an arrow. Type only: no photos, no wordmark artwork, no other decoration. The typeset "Q THREE INTERIORS" line is the one exception to rule 3 and belongs to this template only. Make one with `npm run ad:new -- bold-black ads/work/<slug> ...` (see the skill). When a headline line is too wide, lower `--headline-size` for that ad (never squeeze the tracking) and say so; never rewrite supplied words to fit. Animated: add `--loop 8000`; the rule draws, the headline lines arrive one at a time, then the supporting line, then the button, settled by 36% of the loop and held to the end.
